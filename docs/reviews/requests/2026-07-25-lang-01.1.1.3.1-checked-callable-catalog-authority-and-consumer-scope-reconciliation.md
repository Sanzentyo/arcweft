## Why reconciliation is required

The accepted callable catalog already owns exact callable declaration identity,
`CallableSignatureSchema`, signature source, documentation, access/publication
metadata, provider provenance, and registered project/environment records. The
original Lang-01.1.1.3 request says not to redesign that shared catalog and not
to introduce a copied authority.

The returned package nevertheless defines `CheckedCallableFacts` with owned
`signature: CallableSignatureSchema` and owned `source: CallableSource`, plus
access and execution facts. It never names `RegisteredCallableCatalog`,
`CallableRecord`, or `RegisteredSemanticWorld`, and it specifies neither:

- an exact retained reference from checked facts to the accepted callable
  record; nor
- an atomic replacement that migrates every accepted catalog responsibility
  and deletes the former catalog.

Implementing those shapes literally would leave two signature/source
authorities. Choosing an overlay or a full replacement locally would guess a
public semantic owner and consumer migration. The package's
`READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION` status is therefore not accepted until this single
boundary is corrected.

## Required decisions

### 1. Select one callable-record authority

Choose exactly one final model and give exact Rust-shaped fields, constructors,
visibility, and crate ownership:

1. an effect-checking layer whose checked facts retain or reference the exact
   accepted `Arc<CallableRecord>` and delegate signature/source/documentation/
   access/provenance reads to it; or
2. an in-place elevation/replacement of the accepted catalog that migrates all
   current record responsibilities and deletes the former public owner in the
   same authority switch.

Do not leave both catalogs owning copied `CallableSignatureSchema`, source
spans, documentation, visibility, provider identity, or publication metadata.
Do not introduce a third registry, adapter DTO, compatibility view, or
field-by-field synchronization pass.

### 2. Close construction, transaction, and sharing order

Specify the exact order for:

- accepted project/environment callable registration;
- pending checked shells;
- body/effect inference and trait conformance;
- final immutable catalog publication;
- `TypeCheckReport` and `ProjectSemanticIndex` construction; and
- compiler, LSP, Agent, and persistence consumption.

State which exact `Arc` is shared at each boundary, how stale source/project
revisions fail closed, and what rollback removes. Pending builder state may
exist privately, but no partial or second public reader may be visible.

### 3. Preserve structural and checked identity without a dual resolver

The returned package correctly retains existing `CallableDeclarationId` inside
`CallableDeclarationKey::Existing` and binds it to a revision-aware
`CheckedCallableId`. Specify precisely:

- the final key and value types for `ProjectCallableSymbol`,
  `ProjectGraphSymbolRef`, and project relations;
- whether durable Agent graph identifiers expose structural identity, checked
  identity, or a revision-bound projection, and how they are validated;
- how LSP resolves a source location to the checked record without a spelling
  fallback; and
- which structural identity remains in persistent interface summaries, whose
  durable signature digest is required, without serializing a transient
  checked-context handle.

No consumer may copy the effect row into its own record. No consumer may fall
back from a missing/stale checked record to raw HIR, source text, declaration
name, or a reconstructed signature.

### 4. Reconcile exact current consumers

Give a consumer table covering at least:

- registered project and environment callables;
- ordinary Function and View project symbols;
- trait requirements, trait implementation methods, and inherent methods;
- direct and bound method call facts;
- effect graph edges and conformances;
- `TypeCheckReport` and `ProjectSemanticIndex`;
- accepted LSP hover/signature/navigation/diagnostics;
- Agent project graph/function payloads;
- compiler persistent `InterfaceSummary`; and
- runtime trait-method lowering.

For each row identify the sole record owner, retained identity, and whether the
consumer stores an ID/reference or merely projects output.

### 5. Give a deletion-driven public switch

Provide one compile-clean order that first deletes or makes uncallable the old
row copies, string/local-index identities, raw-HIR signature reconstruction,
and name fallbacks, then fixes the resulting consumers toward the selected
single owner. Preserve the accepted Lang-01.1.1.3 deletions:

- `TraitCallableId`;
- resolver-created empty trait-method rows;
- copied requirement-as-implementation rows;
- string/source callable effect IDs;
- project method-value rejection;
- legacy generic `AWF-EFX-001` / `UpperBoundExceeded` path;
- local-index/string runtime trait identity; and
- `(usize, String)` witness-method lookup.

Do not prescribe a compatibility alias, dual reader, source gate, or permanent
removed-syntax diagnostic during the migration.
