## Why this correction is required

The returned AW-AH-009.3.3.3 package classifies all 23 families into 20
`RejectingSchema` and three `IntentionallyUnchecked` rows. Two rejecting rows
are not valid final migration evidence:

- `CapacityMethod` uses a one-arity `reserve` fixture and treats authored
  spread as a rejection; and
- `Dialogue` uses a `DialogueCallableId::SpeakerLine` fixture described as the
  current authoritative typed owner.

The returned Speaker decision is truthful only as a current-phase observation.
It is not a final family row: the accepted AW-AH-009.4 chain replaces the
current Speaker callable model with Dialogue `CharacterFactory` and
`CharacterReconfigure` and deletes Speaker/SpeakerPreset identities in the
same public switch.

The CapacityMethod row conflicts with accepted AW-AH-009.3.3.4. Capacity
arguments are normatively `variadic_unchecked`: zero, one, multiple, named,
spread, and recovered entries are accepted and checked without an expected
type. The current `homogeneous(..., Named("_"), ...)` schema and its spread
rejection are implementation drift that the typed associated-callee switch
must delete, not truthful negative evidence. CapacityMethod therefore becomes
`IntentionallyUnchecked` when that switch is production authority. Before the
switch, neither the legacy string success dispatcher nor its drifted schema may
earn final migration evidence.

The returned Dialogue row is not valid final migration evidence. Current
`SpeakerLine`/string-content-call/`HirDialogue` production carriers are the
frozen execution path that AW-AH-009.4.2 and AW-AH-009.4.3 replace. They are not
the accepted final ordinary-call or content-application owner. The final
Dialogue content application must join the Proof typed HIR expression arena,
project line-identity transaction, semantic result, and runtime-plan authority
switch. Counting the frozen carrier would preserve the exact obsolete boundary
that the later switch must delete. Before that switch, the contract must not
fabricate a Dialogue candidate from an ordinary call that lacks the final
`CharacterFactory`/`CharacterReconfigure` authority, or encode
`ContentApplication` as `Expr::Call`. After the switch, `CharacterFactory` and
`CharacterReconfigure` legitimately use the existing ordinary `Expr::Call`;
only bracket/colon `ContentApplication` remains a distinct typed HIR operation.

The truthful classification is time-dependent until both public authority
switches land. It also has two distinct evidence axes: current-phase executable
shared-resolver observations may include the obsolete Speaker path, while
final-model completion evidence never may.

Speaker is therefore current-phase evidence only. The atomic Dialogue switch
deletes `SpeakerCallableId`, `CallableFamily::Speaker`, Speaker/SpeakerPreset
types, and the frozen carrier while activating final Dialogue evidence. There
is no valid phase in which both Speaker and final Dialogue receive matrix
credit.

Final Dialogue validation is already fixed by the accepted AW-AH-009.4 chain.
`CharacterFactory` and `CharacterReconfigure` are legitimate ordinary
`Expr::Call` operations with exact/dependent fields, named-only standard
coordinates, `OpenChecked` custom fields, and spread rejection.
`ContentApplication` is the typed bracket/colon HIR operation and must not be
faked as an ordinary call. The remaining design choice is the exact
representative accepted/negative pair and typed observation point for section
19, not whether final Dialogue can reject.

A second conflict concerns the phrase "checked exactly once." The accepted
AW-AH-009.3.3 overload algorithm checks candidate-mapped expressions with each
candidate's exact expected type inside rollback transactions and then retains
or replays the selected/diagnostic transaction. The returned .3.3.3 package
correctly describes a transaction-aware multiset for committed or recovery
facts, but retains the broad `argument_expression_checks`/"exactly once"
wording. That evidence cannot truthfully prove one physical traversal when two
or more candidates were contextually checked.

The final contract must distinguish physical candidate evaluation from
retained committed/recovery inference. It must preserve contextual enum
shorthand, unsuffixed numeric fallback and lowering evidence, closures and
partial placeholders, generic substitution, nested calls with expected
results, fixed-literal spread slots, effects, diagnostics, cancellation, work
charging, and rollback. It must not infer a context-free type cache or an
undefined constraint IR merely to manufacture a physical-once count.
