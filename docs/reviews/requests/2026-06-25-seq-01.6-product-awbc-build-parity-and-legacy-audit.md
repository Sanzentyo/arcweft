# Seq-01.6 Product AWBC Build Wiring, Runtime Parity Review, and Legacy Audit

## Sequence Position

This request follows the implemented seq-01.5 product AWFB bytecode migration.
Seq-01.5 changed product AWFB decoding and product runtime routing so decoded
products require canonical AWBC executable payloads instead of structured
`BytecodeProgram` product payloads.

Seq-01.6 must review and design the remaining work needed to make that
migration complete from the producer side and safe at the product runtime
surface:

- normal bundle builders must emit `product_awbc` before writing product
  `.awfb` bytes;
- compact AWBC product execution must have a complete `RuntimeStepResult`
  parity contract or a clearly documented block;
- remaining structured/compact legacy paths must be classified as product
  blockers, dev/source-only compatibility, or deletion-gated residue.

Do not redesign already implemented and verified seq-01.1 through seq-01.5
substrate unless current repository evidence shows a concrete flaw. In
particular, do not replace the seq-01.5 AWBC-only product payload shape merely
to make builder wiring easier.

## Why This Request Is Needed

Repository review after seq-01.5 found that product codec/runtime source gates
no longer show direct structured product payload fallback in the focused product
files. However, the broader seq-01 migration is not fully closed:

- `crates/arcweft-cli/src/app/bundle.rs::compile_bundle_for_selection` still
  constructs `ArcweftBundle::new(..., compiled.bytecode, ...)` and does not
  attach `with_product_awbc(...)`.
- project and watch bundle paths call `to_format_bytes(BundleFormat::Awfb)` on
  bundles produced by that path, so ordinary product AWFB generation may fail
  with missing product AWBC diagnostics or remain under-tested.
- `docs/reviews/requests/2026-06-25-seq-01.4.1-compact-awbc-runtime-step-parity.md`
  remains open as the design request for complete compact AWBC
  `RuntimeStepResult` parity.
- `arcweft-core::compact_bytecode`, structured VM/AOT compatibility tiers, and
  structured `bundle.bytecode.program` inspection/dev paths still exist and
  need an explicit product/deletion classification.

This request asks for a concrete design and review package, not a broad
architecture rewrite.

## Required Decisions

1. Decide which crate or context owns production `RuntimePlan` /
   `BytecodeProgram` to `AwbcProgram` lowering for product bundle builds.
   Prefer an existing compiler/lowering context when interning, diagnostics,
   source maps, or policy are needed.
2. Define the exact typed API that bundle builders use to attach product AWBC.
   Avoid a scattered helper such as `bytecode_to_awbc` if the behavior belongs
   on an owned compiler output type, lowering context, or bundle builder.
3. Decide whether `CompiledBundleArtifact` should carry both structured
   `BytecodeProgram` and product `AwbcProgram`, or whether `ArcweftBundle::new`
   should receive a product executable object assembled by the compiler layer.
4. Define how build diagnostics report missing or failed AWBC lowering before
   `.awfb` encoding, instead of surfacing only a late
   `MissingProductAwbcExecutable` codec error.
5. Decide whether source/runtime watch bundles are product `.awfb` artifacts or
   dev-only bundles, and whether they must also attach product AWBC.
6. Define the compatibility policy for structured VM/AOT tiers after product
   build wiring lands. State which usages remain dev/source-only and which must
   be removed or blocked for product `.awfb`.
7. Decide whether seq-01.4.1 runtime-step parity must be completed before
   ordinary product builders emit AWBC-only `.awfb`, or whether builder wiring
   may land behind an explicit opt-in gate.
8. Define the deletion gate for `arcweft-core::compact_bytecode` using current
   import evidence, product fixture coverage, and source gates.
9. Define how AWBC product build output participates in patch/generation
   identity, signing/hash identity, source maps, display maps, and resource
   consistency tests.

## Implementation Order To Specify

1. **Build-path inventory and failing reproduction**
   - Identify every CLI/project/watch/test path that writes
     `BundleFormat::Awfb`.
   - Add or specify a focused failing test that exercises ordinary product AWFB
     generation without manually calling `with_product_awbc`.
   - The test should fail for the current reason before the fix, not by relying
     on unrelated filesystem or environment behavior.

2. **Typed product AWBC build API**
   - Introduce the smallest stable typed interface for product AWBC lowering
     and bundle attachment.
   - Keep `arcweft-bundle` as a data/codec crate; do not make it depend on the
     compiler or filesystem.
   - Keep compiler/lowering diagnostics structured and source-aware where
     source spans are available.

3. **Builder wiring**
   - Wire `compile_bundle_for_selection`, project bundle generation, watch
     bundle output, and CLI tests through the product AWBC build API.
   - Do not add a silent structured product fallback.
   - If a dev path intentionally writes non-product structured bundles, name
     that mode explicitly and keep it out of product `.awfb` defaults.

4. **Runtime-step parity review**
   - Review `arcweft-core::awbc::product_step` against seq-01.4.1.
   - Either implement the missing parity needed for product-default execution
     or update seq-01.4.1 with exact blockers that must be designed first.
   - At minimum, classify dialogue, choice, await, await-many, host-call,
     source, stream, budget-yield, traps, source-map reporting, and pure-helper
     stats.

5. **Legacy classification and deletion gates**
   - Classify each remaining reference to `compact_bytecode`,
     `BytecodeVmExecutor`, `StructuredVm`, `StructuredAot`,
     `from_bytecode_parts`, and `bundle.bytecode.program`.
   - Mark each as product blocker, dev/source compatibility, test fixture, or
     deletion-gated residue.
   - Delete only when the gate is satisfied; otherwise document the exact gate
     in `docs/implementation/`.

6. **Product fixture and identity coverage**
   - Add AWBC-only product build fixtures from source.
   - Add tests for malformed/missing AWBC diagnostics, patch/generation digest
     changes, signing/hash identity if available in this layer, and runtime
     smoke through runtime-driver/runtime-host/native-player as allowed by
     seq-01.4.1 parity.

7. **Documentation and audit**
   - Update `docs/implementation/` with the current completion boundary,
     excluded non-goals, and verification results.
   - Update stable design docs only when the product build or product format
     contract changes.
   - Run the structural audit at the reviewable cut point.

## Tests To Require

The design must name exact tests or fixture families for:

- ordinary CLI/project AWFB build emits `product_awbc`;
- ordinary CLI/project AWFB build no longer fails with missing product AWBC;
- watch/run bundle output either emits product AWBC or is explicitly marked
  dev-only and tested as such;
- AWFB bytes produced from source decode as AWBC-only product bundles;
- structured product payloads remain rejected with typed diagnostics;
- malformed and missing AWBC diagnostics remain stable;
- product runtime smoke uses `ArcweftExecutionTier::AwbcProduct` for decoded
  product bundles;
- dialogue, choice, await, await-many, host-call, source, stream,
  budget-yield, trap, and source-map parity are covered or explicitly blocked
  by seq-01.4.1;
- patch/generation identity changes when canonical AWBC bytes change;
- source gates prove product paths do not construct `BytecodeVmExecutor`
  directly or read structured product payloads as executable truth;
- `compact_bytecode` has no non-test product imports before deletion.

## Constraints

- Keep `arcweft-core`, `arcweft-bundle`, and other data-format crates Sans I/O.
- Do not make `arcweft-bundle` depend on compiler, CLI, runtime-host,
  filesystem, network, signing keys, clocks, or platform adapters.
- Do not couple product players to compiler-only crates.
- Prefer typed APIs and owned methods/context objects over free-standing
  endpoint-named conversion helpers.
- Do not add compatibility shims, aliases, or silent fallback branches that
  accept removed product syntax or structured product payloads.
- Do not treat broad seq-01 intent as sufficient design for runtime parity;
  map each suspension/observation family explicitly.
- Preserve crate layering: syntax -> HIR -> sema -> runtime-plan/AWBC lowering
  -> bundle data/codec -> runtime-driver/host/player adapters.
- Keep implementation-state notes in `docs/implementation/`; stable design
  chapters should record durable product/build contracts only.

## Non-Goals

- Redesigning canonical AWBC encoding or the seq-01.5 AWBC-only product payload
  marker without concrete evidence that the current format is wrong.
- Implementing optimized native/AOT compiled regions.
- Adding one-shot external migration tooling for old products unless a separate
  request makes it implementation-ready.
- Preserving structured product bytecode as an indefinite product fallback.
- Deleting structured VM/AOT dev tiers merely because they are legacy; deletion
  must follow product impact and test coverage evidence.
