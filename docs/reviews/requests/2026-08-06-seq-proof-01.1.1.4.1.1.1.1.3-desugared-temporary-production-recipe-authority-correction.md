## Why this correction is required

The accepted v6.1.1.4.1.1.1.1 package retains this structural row:

```text
role:                 DesugaredTemporary
owners:               Expr | Stmt
ordinal domain:       0..=1_023
canonical order:      source-causing token order, then immutable recipe-step order
source anchor:        end of the source operator/token requiring desugaring
required boundary:    1,024 commits; the 1,025th rolls back atomically
```

Its generator evidence additionally requires a real production recipe
descriptor, attached Expr/Stmt plans, a real production cursor, typed temporary
payloads, source rows, consumers, and order-perturbation tests. The package does
not define the production recipe schema, which current language constructs
produce it, how a temporary is referenced by retained HIR, or which consumer
uses it.

The repository evidence inspected at committed `main`
`f587e75750d9c5d9b6d8c84e0f098a4cfa80f68b` is decisive about the present gap:

- `SyntheticRole::DesugaredTemporary` exists in
  `crates/arcweft-lang-hir/src/identity.rs`, with owner admission and fingerprint
  tag `0x07`;
- non-test `crates/arcweft-lang-hir/src/final_lowering/**` contains zero
  `DesugaredTemporary` producer references;
- production `arcweft-lang-hir` and `arcweft-lang-syntax` contain zero recipe
  descriptor, temporary-producing step, or recipe-cursor authority matching the
  accepted contract; and
- `ExpressionProjection::Pipe` lowers directly to the retained typed
  `HirExprKind::Pipe(HirPipeExpr { left, right })`. The accepted final leaf
  model and current production do not establish Pipe as a desugaring recipe.

The implementation worktree used to discover this gap was dirty at the audit:
branch `codex/proof-public-switch`, committed base
`f587e75750d9c5d9b6d8c84e0f098a4cfa80f68b`, with 1,284 changed paths
(718 modified, 356 deleted, and 210 untracked). Those uncommitted files and any
test-only synthetic reservation are diagnostic evidence only. They are not an
accepted revision, cannot be required by a GitHub-only designer, and must not
be cited as proof that a production recipe exists.

The final authority must therefore choose and completely specify one of two
outcomes:

1. retain `DesugaredTemporary` because concrete accepted production constructs
   require it, and define their complete real recipe/temporary authority; or
2. delete the role and all associated fingerprint/matrix/counter claims because
   no accepted production construct owns such a recipe.

`Reserved for future use`, a test-only fake cursor, and an inferred Pipe
desugaring are not implementation-ready outcomes.

### 2. Complete retained-role authority, if `RETAIN`

If the role remains, provide a complete schema and producer table that can be
implemented without invention. At minimum close:

- every producing typed syntax projection and final-HIR construct;
- the exact Expr or Stmt owner reserved before temporary allocation;
- the owner-local immutable recipe descriptor and its stable typed recipe/step
  identity;
- the attached Expr/Stmt plan fields that bind source-causing token events to
  that recipe;
- whether one source event may invoke several steps and how a step declares
  its child `HirIdKind` and payload constructor;
- the exact temporary child payload for every step and the typed reference by
  which later parent/child HIR, sema, runtime-plan, compiler, or tooling
  consumers use it;
- scope, visibility, liveness interval, retry/reuse, and retirement behavior;
- the sole source-manifest rows, insertion anchors, poison/diagnostic behavior,
  and query applicability;
- deterministic ordering by attached source event followed by immutable recipe
  step order, including stable tie-breaking without map/vector iteration;
- checked ordinal/counter arithmetic and interaction with
  `SyntheticDescendantsPerOwner`, expression/statement arena limits, source
  component limits, diagnostics, and total-slot accounting;
- exact publication, rollback, retry, and cache invalidation behavior; and
- every production consumer and the deletion of any obsolete reader or
  provisional helper during the authority switch.

Define the real production cursor API and ownership. Direct
`reserve_synthetic`, a test-only source scan for an operator, a hand-built
recipe plan, or a fixture-only temporary payload cannot satisfy this decision.
No recipe field may store source strings, reconstructed ranges, raw IDs,
sentinels, or an untyped callback.
