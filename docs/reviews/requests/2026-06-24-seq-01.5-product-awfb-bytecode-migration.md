# Request 01.5: Product AWFB Bytecode Migration

## Sequence Position

Submit this last in the Request 01 branch, after lowering, compact VM parity,
compiled-region fallback, and runtime-driver/runtime-host migration are
designed.

## Request

Please design migration of product AWFB payloads from structured
`BytecodeProgram` runtime sections to canonical AWBC-only executable payloads,
including deletion criteria for the old `compact_bytecode` sidecar and
structured product execution path.

## Why This Needs A Separate Design Cut

The executable-runtime-core overlay lists migration gates, but it does not
fully design the product container transition, compatibility policy, fixture
corpus, deletion order, or user-facing diagnostics for AWFB decode/build
changes.

This must be designed separately because changing product payloads before
runtime parity is stable would make regressions hard to isolate.

## Required Design Decisions

Please provide concrete answers for:

1. What is the current AWFB/product section layout for structured
   `BytecodeProgram`, AWBC, resources, manifests, source maps, display maps, and
   old compact sidecars?
2. What is the target product section layout when AWBC is the sole executable
   payload?
3. How should builders encode AWBC and reject or omit structured bytecode?
4. How should decoders handle old products during the migration window?
5. What exact versioning, manifest, or capability markers distinguish old and
   new payloads?
6. What diagnostics are emitted when a player sees unsupported structured-only,
   malformed AWBC, missing maps, or mismatched resources?
7. What deletion gates remove structured product payload conversions,
   structured product VM execution, and `arcweft-core::compact_bytecode`?
8. What fixture corpus proves product decode and execution parity before and
   after migration?
9. How do product signing, resource sections, patch manifests, and future
   generation updates interact with AWBC identity?
10. What documentation updates are required for builders, players, and archive
    compatibility?

## Required Implementation Order In The Design

Please propose small cuts in this order or justify a better order:

1. Inventory current product bytecode sections and decode/build entrypoints.
2. Add AWBC product section encode/decode tests while still accepting old
   payloads explicitly.
3. Switch builders to produce AWBC as the primary executable section.
4. Switch players to require AWBC for new products.
5. Add migration diagnostics and old-product fixture coverage.
6. Delete structured product payload conversions after gates pass.
7. Delete old `compact_bytecode` sidecar after no product/runtime path uses it.

## Tests To Specify

The design should include tests for:

- AWFB decode of AWBC-only products;
- explicit rejection or migration of structured-only products;
- malformed AWBC product diagnostics;
- resource/source/display map consistency;
- product signing/hash identity stability;
- patch/generation identity compatibility;
- deletion-gate checks proving no product path depends on structured
  `BytecodeProgram` or old compact sidecars.

## Constraints

- Do not preserve both product executable models as silent compatibility layers.
- Keep migration compatibility explicit, documented, and deletion-gated.
- Keep data-format crates Sans I/O.
- Do not couple product players to compiler-only crates.

## Expected Output

Please produce one design document with:

- current and target product section schemas;
- versioning/compatibility policy;
- decode/build diagnostics;
- migration and deletion gates;
- interactions with signing, resources, patches, and generation identity;
- implementation cuts;
- focused tests;
- explicit non-goals.
